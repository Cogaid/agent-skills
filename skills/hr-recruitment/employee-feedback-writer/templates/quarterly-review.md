## Reviewer Checklist (Complete Before Submitting)

- [ ] I have cited specific, observable evidence for each rating
- [ ] I have used SBI format for accomplishments and development areas
- [ ] I have reviewed the full period (not just recent weeks)
- [ ] I have calibrated my ratings against the rating definitions
- [ ] I have set SMART goals for next quarter
- [ ] I have identified development actions with timelines
- [ ] No surprises: all feedback has been discussed previously in 1:1s
- [ ] I would give the same rating to any employee with identical performance
