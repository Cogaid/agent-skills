# Candidate Communication Templates

## Advance to Next Stage

### Email: Phone Screen Invitation

```
Subject: Next Steps - [Position Title] at [Company Name]

Dear [Candidate Name],

Thank you for your application for the [Position Title] role at [Company Name].

After reviewing your background, we were impressed by your [specific relevant
experience, e.g., "experience building scalable data pipelines at Acme Corp"]
and would like to move forward with the next step in our process.

We would like to schedule a [30/45]-minute phone screen to discuss the role
in more detail and learn more about your experience.

Please [select a time that works for you using this link: [scheduling link] /
reply with your availability for the next week].

If you have any questions in the meantime, please do not hesitate to reach out.

Looking forward to connecting,

[Recruiter Name]
[Title]
[Company Name]
[Phone] | [Email]
```

## Decline

### Email: Respectful Rejection (Standard)

```
Subject: Your Application to [Company Name]

Dear [Candidate Name],

Thank you for your interest in the [Position Title] position at [Company Name]
and for taking the time to submit your application.

After careful review, we have decided to move forward with candidates whose
experience more closely aligns with our current needs for this specific role.

This decision does not reflect on your qualifications or potential. We
encourage you to apply for future positions that match your skills and
experience. We will keep your information on file for [12 months / future
consideration].

We wish you the best in your career search.

Sincerely,
[Recruiter Name]
[Company Name]
```

### Email: Rejection with Encouragement (Strong Candidate, Wrong Role)

```
Subject: Your Application to [Company Name]

Dear [Candidate Name],

Thank you for applying for the [Position Title] role at [Company Name].
We appreciated learning about your background in [specific area].

While we have decided to move forward with other candidates for this
particular role, your profile stood out to us. We think your experience
in [specific skill/area] could be a strong fit for future opportunities
on our team.

Would you be open to us reaching out if a more aligned role opens up?
In the meantime, I encourage you to keep an eye on our careers page at
[URL].

Thank you again for your interest in [Company Name].

Best regards,
[Recruiter Name]
[Company Name]
```

### Email: Rejection After Hold (Borderline Candidate)

```
Subject: Update on Your Application - [Position Title]

Dear [Candidate Name],

Thank you for your patience as we completed our review for the [Position
Title] position at [Company Name].

After thorough consideration, we have decided to proceed with candidates
whose backgrounds more directly match our current requirements. We want
you to know that your application received careful attention and this
was not an easy decision.

We genuinely appreciate your interest in [Company Name] and encourage
you to apply for future roles that align with your experience.

Wishing you all the best,
[Recruiter Name]
[Company Name]
```

## Batch Processing Notes

### Volume Screening Communication Cadence

| Application Age | Action | Template |
|----------------|--------|----------|
| 0-3 days | Auto-acknowledgment | "We received your application" |
| 3-7 days | Screening complete | Advance / Decline / Hold decision |
| 7-14 days | Hold resolution | Final advance or decline |
| 14+ days without response | Candidate experience risk | Send status update regardless |

### Communication Best Practices

1. **Respond within 7 business days** -- even if the answer is "we are still reviewing"
2. **Never ghost candidates** -- every applicant deserves a response
3. **Personalize when possible** -- mention something specific from their resume
4. **Be prompt with advances** -- top candidates have multiple options; speed is competitive advantage
5. **Be kind with rejections** -- today's rejected candidate is tomorrow's customer, referral source, or perfect fit for another role
