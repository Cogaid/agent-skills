# Offer Letter Writer

Draft clear, compliant, and compelling offer letters with structured compensation packages, negotiation frameworks, and verbal offer scripts.

## Purpose

The offer letter is the final critical touchpoint before a candidate decides to join. A well-crafted offer communicates professionalism, transparency, and excitement while protecting the company legally. This skill produces offer letters tailored to role level, includes total compensation breakdowns, and provides guidance for negotiation scenarios.

## Quick Reference

### Offer Letter Structure

| Section | Required | Purpose |
|---------|----------|---------|
| Header / Date | Yes | Company letterhead, date, candidate address |
| Greeting | Yes | Personal, warm opening |
| Position and Start Date | Yes | Title, department, reporting structure, proposed start date |
| Compensation | Yes | Base salary, pay frequency, currency |
| Bonus / Variable Pay | If applicable | Target bonus, commission structure, performance criteria |
| Equity | If applicable | Grant type, vesting schedule, strike price (if options) |
| Benefits Summary | Yes | Health, retirement, PTO, and key perks |
| Employment Terms | Yes | At-will status, employment type, work location |
| Contingencies | Yes | Background check, references, right to work |
| Acceptance Deadline | Yes | Typically 3-7 business days |
| Signature Block | Yes | Authorized signer and candidate signature lines |
| Appendices | Optional | Detailed benefits guide, equity agreement, NDA |

## Workflow

### Step 1: Pre-Offer Preparation

- [ ] Confirm final compensation approval with hiring manager and comp team
- [ ] Verify budget availability and headcount approval
- [ ] Review internal equity (compare to existing team members at same level)
- [ ] Check market data for the role, location, and level
- [ ] Determine negotiation flexibility and walkaway thresholds
- [ ] Prepare competing offer analysis if candidate has alternatives

### Step 2: Verbal Offer (Before Written)

- [ ] Schedule a call with the candidate (do not make offers via email first)
- [ ] Use the verbal offer script (see below)
- [ ] Gauge candidate enthusiasm and potential concerns
- [ ] Address initial questions about compensation, role, or logistics
- [ ] Confirm the candidate wants to receive the written offer
- [ ] Set expectations for offer letter delivery timeline

### Step 3: Draft Written Offer

- [ ] Select appropriate template based on role level
- [ ] Fill in all compensation components
- [ ] Include specific contingencies and conditions
- [ ] Add acceptance deadline (3-7 business days standard)
- [ ] Review with legal or HR for compliance
- [ ] Send via secure method with clear instructions

### Step 4: Negotiation (If Applicable)

- [ ] Listen to the candidate's counteroffer without immediate response
- [ ] Assess which components are flexible (see negotiation ranges below)
- [ ] Prepare counter-proposal within approved bounds
- [ ] Present adjusted offer with clear rationale
- [ ] Document final agreed terms

### Step 5: Acceptance and Transition

- [ ] Receive signed offer letter
- [ ] Send confirmation and welcome materials
- [ ] Initiate background check and other contingencies
- [ ] Coordinate with onboarding team
- [ ] Notify other finalists respectfully

## Templates

### Standard Offer Letter

```
[COMPANY LETTERHEAD]

[Date]

[Candidate Full Name]
[Candidate Address]
[City, State ZIP]

Dear [Candidate First Name],

On behalf of [Company Name], I am thrilled to extend the following offer
of employment. We were impressed by your [specific quality from interview]
and believe you will make a significant impact on our [team/mission].

POSITION DETAILS
  Title: [Job Title]
  Department: [Department]
  Reporting To: [Manager Name, Title]
  Location: [Office Location / Remote / Hybrid]
  Start Date: [Proposed Start Date]
  Employment Type: [Full-time, Exempt]

COMPENSATION
  Base Salary: $[Amount] per year, paid [bi-weekly/semi-monthly]
  Signing Bonus: $[Amount], payable within [30] days of start date
    (subject to [12]-month clawback if voluntary departure)
  Annual Bonus: Target of [X]% of base salary, based on individual
    and company performance, prorated for partial first year

[IF EQUITY]
EQUITY
  Grant Type: [RSU / Stock Options]
  Number of Shares: [X,XXX]
  Vesting Schedule: [4-year vest with 1-year cliff, monthly thereafter]
  [For Options: Strike Price: $X.XX per share]
  Subject to the terms of the [Company Name] Equity Incentive Plan
  and a separate equity agreement

BENEFITS
  You will be eligible for [Company Name]'s benefits package, including:
  - Medical, dental, and vision insurance (effective [date])
  - [401(k) / pension] plan with [X]% company match
  - [X] days of paid time off per year
  - [X] paid holidays
  - [Additional benefits: parental leave, wellness stipend, etc.]

  A detailed benefits guide is attached as Appendix A.

EMPLOYMENT TERMS
  This offer is for at-will employment, meaning either you or [Company
  Name] may end the employment relationship at any time, with or without
  cause or notice. This letter is not a contract of employment for any
  specific period.

CONTINGENCIES
  This offer is contingent upon:
  - Satisfactory completion of a background check
  - Verification of your legal right to work in [country]
  - [Any additional contingencies: reference check, drug screen, etc.]

ACCEPTANCE
  To accept this offer, please sign below and return this letter by
  [Deadline Date]. If you have any questions, please do not hesitate
  to contact me at [phone] or [email].

We are excited about the possibility of you joining [Company Name] and
look forward to your response.

Sincerely,

_________________________________
[Authorized Signer Name]
[Authorized Signer Title]
[Company Name]

ACCEPTANCE

I, [Candidate Name], accept the offer of employment as described above.

Signature: _________________________  Date: _______________

Print Name: ________________________
```

### Executive Offer Letter Additions

```
EXECUTIVE COMPENSATION ADDENDUM

SEVERANCE

  subject to execution of a separation agreement.

CHANGE OF CONTROL
  In the event of a change of control, [X]% of unvested equity will
  accelerate. [Double-trigger: acceleration requires both change of
  control and termination within [X] months.]

EXECUTIVE BENEFITS
  - Executive health plan
  - Financial planning allowance: $[X]/year
  - Executive coaching: [X] sessions/year
  - Board observer rights [if applicable]
  - [D&O insurance coverage]

NON-COMPETE / NON-SOLICITATION
  Subject to a separate [Non-Compete / Non-Solicitation] agreement
  with a [X]-month post-employment restriction period, attached as
  Appendix B.
```

### Verbal Offer Script

```
"Hi [Candidate Name], thank you for making time for this call. I have
some exciting news.

After [thorough deliberation / enthusiastic discussion] among the
interview team, we would like to extend you an offer to join [Company
Name] as [Title] on the [Team] team.

Before I go into the details, I want to say that [specific positive
feedback from interviews - e.g., 'your approach to the system design
challenge really stood out' or 'the team was impressed by your
leadership examples'].

Here is what we are proposing:

- Base salary of $[X]
- [Signing bonus of $X]
- [Annual bonus target of X%]
- [Equity grant of X,XXX shares vesting over 4 years]
- Full benefits including [highlight 2-3 key benefits]
- Start date of [Date]

How does that sound to you? I want to make sure this aligns with
your expectations.

[LISTEN - do not fill silence]

I will send the formal offer letter [today/tomorrow] with all the
details in writing. You will have [X] business days to review and
respond. Of course, I am available for any questions in the meantime.

Is there anything you would like to discuss now?"
```

## Legal Requirements Checklist

| Requirement | Jurisdiction | Notes |
|------------|-------------|-------|
| At-will language | Most US states | Montana is the exception |
| Salary transparency | CA, CO, NY, WA + others | Must include pay range in posting and/or offer |
| Salary history ban | 20+ US states and cities | Cannot ask for or use prior salary |
| Non-compete restrictions | CA, MN, ND, OK ban them; FTC proposed federal ban | Verify state law before including |
| Background check disclosure | FCRA (federal), state ban-the-box laws | Must disclose and get consent |
| Benefits waiting period | ACA requirements | Max 90-day waiting period for health coverage |
| I-9 / Right to work | Federal (US) | Cannot specify visa type; must verify post-hire |
| Equal pay | Federal + state laws | Offer must be equitable across protected classes |

## Best Practices

1. **Always make the verbal offer before the written one.** Gauge enthusiasm and address concerns before formalizing.
2. **Lead with excitement, not paperwork.** The offer call should feel like good news, not a contract negotiation.
3. **Be transparent about total compensation.** Candidates who understand the full value of the package are more likely to accept.
4. **Leave room for negotiation.** If your initial offer is your best and final, you leave no room for the candidate to feel they advocated for themselves.
5. **Respond to counteroffers within 24-48 hours.** Delays signal disinterest or dysfunction.
6. **Never rescind verbally offered terms in the written letter.** This destroys trust.
7. **Check internal equity.** Ensure the offer does not create pay disparities with existing team members at the same level.
8. **Set a reasonable deadline.** 3-5 business days for individual contributors; 5-7 for executives. Accommodate personal circumstances.
9. **Document everything.** Keep records of all compensation discussions for compliance and future reference.
10. **Celebrate the acceptance.** Send a welcome message, swag, or team introduction within 24 hours of signed offer.
