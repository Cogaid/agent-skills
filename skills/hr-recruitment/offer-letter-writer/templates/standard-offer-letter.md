# Standard Offer Letter Template

```
[COMPANY LETTERHEAD / LOGO]

[Date]

[Candidate Full Name]
[Candidate Address Line 1]
[City, State ZIP Code]

Dear [Candidate First Name],

On behalf of [Company Name], I am thrilled to extend the following offer of
employment. We were impressed by your [specific quality or interview highlight]
and believe you will make a significant contribution to our [team/department/mission].

---

POSITION DETAILS

  Title:            [Job Title]
  Department:       [Department Name]
  Reporting To:     [Manager Name], [Manager Title]
  Location:         [Office Address / Remote / Hybrid (X days in office)]
  Start Date:       [Proposed Start Date]
  Employment Type:  [Full-time / Part-time], [Exempt / Non-exempt]

---

COMPENSATION

  Base Salary:      $[Amount] per year, paid [bi-weekly / semi-monthly]
                    (equivalent to $[Amount] per pay period before deductions)

  Signing Bonus:    $[Amount], payable within [30] days of your start date
                    Note: If you voluntarily resign within [12] months of your
                    start date, you agree to repay the signing bonus on a
                    prorated basis.

  Annual Bonus:     Target of [X]% of your annualized base salary, based on
                    a combination of individual performance and company results.
                    Bonus for the first year will be prorated based on your
                    start date. Bonus is typically paid in [month] for the
                    prior fiscal year.

---

EQUITY (if applicable -- remove section if not offering equity)

  Grant Type:       [Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) / Incentive Stock Options (ISOs)]
  Number of Shares: [X,XXX] shares
  Vesting Schedule: Four-year vesting with a one-year cliff. After the first
                    anniversary of your start date, 25% of your grant will vest.
                    The remaining shares vest [monthly / quarterly] over the
                    following 36 months.
  [For Options Only]
  Strike Price:     $[X.XX] per share (based on the current 409A valuation)

  This equity grant is subject to the terms and conditions of the [Company Name]
  [Year] Equity Incentive Plan and a separate equity agreement, which will be
  provided to you on or shortly after your start date.

---

BENEFITS

  You will be eligible for [Company Name]'s comprehensive benefits package,
  effective [start date / first of the month following start date]:

  - Medical, dental, and vision insurance
    ([X]% employer-paid for employee; [X]% for dependents)
  - [401(k) / 403(b)] retirement plan with [X]% company match
    (eligible after [X] days)
  - [X] days of paid time off (PTO) per year, accruing from start date
  - [X] paid company holidays per year
  - [X] weeks paid parental leave
  - $[X] annual professional development / learning budget
  - [Additional benefits: wellness stipend, commuter benefits, etc.]

  A detailed benefits guide is available at [link / attached as Appendix A].

---

EMPLOYMENT TERMS

  Your employment with [Company Name] is at-will. This means that either you
  or [Company Name] may terminate the employment relationship at any time, for
  any reason, with or without cause or notice. This letter is not a contract or
  guarantee of employment for any specific duration.

  Nothing in this letter or in any [Company Name] policy or practice shall be
  construed to alter the at-will nature of your employment unless set forth in
  a written agreement signed by the [CEO / authorized officer] of [Company Name].

---

CONTINGENCIES

  This offer is contingent upon the satisfactory completion of:

  - Background verification check
  - Verification of your legal right to work in [the United States / country]
    (Form I-9 completion within 3 business days of start date)
  - [Reference check completion]
  - [Drug screening (if applicable)]
  - [Execution of Confidentiality and Invention Assignment Agreement]
  - [Any role-specific contingencies: security clearance, licensure, etc.]

---

ADDITIONAL AGREEMENTS

  As a condition of employment, you will be required to sign:
  - Confidential Information and Invention Assignment Agreement
  - [Non-solicitation Agreement (if applicable)]
  - [Arbitration Agreement (if applicable)]
  - Employee Handbook acknowledgment

  Copies of these agreements are [attached / will be provided on Day 1].

---

ACCEPTANCE

  To accept this offer, please sign and date below and return this letter
  by [Deadline Date -- typically 5 business days from send date].

  You may return the signed letter via:
  - Email: [HR email] (scanned signature or e-signature accepted)
  - Mail: [Company address]

  If you have any questions about this offer or need additional time to
  consider, please contact me directly at [phone] or [email]. I am happy
  to discuss any aspect of this offer.

---

We are genuinely excited about the possibility of you joining [Company Name].
Your [specific quality] will be a tremendous addition to our team, and we look
forward to the impact we can make together.

Welcome aboard (almost)!

Sincerely,


_________________________________
[Authorized Signer Name]
[Authorized Signer Title]
[Company Name]
[Date]


===================================================================

OFFER ACCEPTANCE

I, [Candidate Full Name], accept the offer of employment at [Company Name]
as described in this letter. I understand that my employment is at-will and
that this letter does not constitute a contract for any specific period.

I confirm that I am not subject to any non-compete or other agreement that
would prevent me from performing my duties at [Company Name], or if I am,
I have disclosed such agreements to [Company Name].


Signature: _________________________________  Date: _______________

Print Name: ________________________________


===================================================================

OFFER DECLINE (if applicable)

I, [Candidate Full Name], respectfully decline this offer of employment.

Reason (optional): _____________________________________________


Signature: _________________________________  Date: _______________
```

---

## Usage Notes

- Replace all bracketed [text] with actual values
- Remove the Equity section entirely if not offering equity
- Remove any contingencies that do not apply
- Adjust at-will language for Montana (good cause required)
- Include salary range disclosure if required by state law
- Have legal/HR review before sending
- Send within 24 hours of verbal acceptance to maintain momentum
