## STAR Scoring Guide

| Score | Situation | Task | Action | Result |
|-------|-----------|------|--------|--------|
| 1 (Weak) | Vague or generic setup | Team effort, unclear personal role | Abstract or hypothetical | No outcome or only positive spin |
| 2 (Adequate) | Clear context with some specificity | Personal responsibility stated | Logical steps described | Outcome mentioned but not quantified |
| 3 (Strong) | Specific, relevant scenario with clear stakes | Distinct personal ownership and initiative | Detailed sequence with decision rationale | Quantified impact + reflection on learning |
