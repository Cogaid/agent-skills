## Debrief Protocol

**Timing:** Same day as final interviews, or within 24 hours
**Required attendees:** All interviewers
**Duration:** 30-45 minutes

### Debrief Agenda

1. **Independent scores submitted** (before the meeting) -- 0 min
2. **Score summary review** -- 5 min
   - Display scores without names of scorers initially
   - Identify consensus and disagreement areas
3. **Each interviewer presents** (2-3 min each) -- 15 min
   - Top strength with evidence
   - Top concern with evidence
   - Recommendation with brief rationale
4. **Discuss disagreements** -- 10 min
   - Focus on evidence, not opinions
   - Facilitator (recruiter) ensures all voices heard
5. **Final decision** -- 5 min
   - Consensus: Strong Hire, Hire, No Hire, Strong No Hire
   - If no consensus: Hiring manager makes final call with documented rationale

### Decision Framework

| Outcome | Criteria | Next Step |
|---------|----------|-----------|
| Strong Hire | Majority strong hire; no strong no hire | Extend offer within 24-48 hours |
| Hire | Majority hire or above; concerns addressable | Extend offer within 3-5 days |
| No Hire | Majority no hire; significant concerns | Respectful decline |
| Strong No Hire | Any strong no hire with substantive evidence | Immediate decline |
| Split Decision | No majority; substantive disagreement | Additional data gathering (reference, additional round) |
