## Survey Analysis Guide

### Key Metrics to Track

| Metric | Calculation | Target | Alert Threshold |
|--------|-------------|--------|----------------|
| Week 1 average score | Mean of Q1+Q4+Q5+Q6 | 4.0+ / 5 | Below 3.5 |
| Equipment readiness rate | % answering "Yes" to Q2 | 95%+ | Below 85% |
| Tool access completeness | % answering "Yes" to Q3 | 95%+ | Below 85% |
| 30-day productivity score | Mean of Q2 | 3.5+ / 5 | Below 3.0 |
| 90-day NPS | Standard NPS calculation | 50+ | Below 30 |
| Time to productivity | Median of Q7 | 4-6 weeks | Above 8 weeks |
| Buddy effectiveness | Mean of buddy rating | 4.0+ / 5 | Below 3.5 |

### Action Triggers

| Survey Score | Action |
|-------------|--------|
| Any question scores 1-2 | Manager and HR notified within 24 hours for immediate follow-up |
| Overall average below 3.0 | Emergency onboarding review; assign additional support |
| NPS below 0 | Systemic review of onboarding program |
| Equipment/access issues | IT process review; preventive measures for next hire |
| Buddy score below 3 | Buddy reassignment; review buddy selection criteria |
