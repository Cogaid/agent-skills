# Product Description Templates

Complete collection of product description templates for various platforms and product types.

---

## Template 1: Standard E-commerce Product Page

```markdown
# [Product Name] -- [Primary Benefit in 5-8 Words]

[Opening paragraph: 2-3 sentences that paint a picture of the problem or
desire, then position the product as the answer. Speak to the customer's
life, not the product's specs.]

## Why You'll Love It

- **[Benefit 1]** -- [Brief explanation tied to a feature]
- **[Benefit 2]** -- [Brief explanation tied to a feature]
- **[Benefit 3]** -- [Brief explanation tied to a feature]
- **[Benefit 4]** -- [Brief explanation tied to a feature]

## What's Included

- [Item 1]
- [Item 2]
- [Item 3]
- [Item 4 -- e.g., carrying case, charger, manual]

## Specifications

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| [Dimension/Size] | [Value] |
| [Weight] | [Value] |
| [Material/Construction] | [Value] |
| [Capacity/Performance] | [Value] |
| [Compatibility] | [Value] |
| [Warranty] | [Value] |

## Perfect For

[1-2 sentences describing the ideal customer or use case. Help the buyer
self-identify: "If you're a [persona] who [does X], this was made for you."]

---

**[Trust signal]** -- [e.g., "30-Day Money-Back Guarantee" or "Free Shipping
on Orders Over $50"]

**[CTA Button Text]** -- [e.g., "Add to Cart" or "Buy Now - $XX.XX"]
```

## Template 2: Amazon Listing

```markdown
TITLE (200 chars max):
[Brand] [Product Name] - [Key Benefit] - [Key Feature] - [Material/Type] -
[Size/Quantity/Color] - [Use Case]

---

BULLET POINTS (5):

- **BENEFIT-FIRST HEADLINE**: [Feature] delivers [benefit] so you can
  [outcome]. [Specific detail that builds credibility -- number, material,
  certification].

- **SECOND BENEFIT**: [Feature] means [advantage]. Unlike [common
  alternative], our [product] [specific differentiator]. [Use case or
  scenario].

- **THIRD BENEFIT**: [Feature] provides [benefit]. Whether you're [use
  case 1] or [use case 2], [product] adapts to your needs. [Supporting
  detail].

- **FOURTH BENEFIT**: [Feature] ensures [benefit]. Tested to [standard]
  and certified by [authority]. [Proof point -- number of units sold,
  years in business, awards].

- **100% SATISFACTION GUARANTEE**: We stand behind every [product]. If
  you're not completely satisfied, [return/refund policy]. [Support
  detail -- e.g., "Our US-based team responds within 24 hours."]

---

PRODUCT DESCRIPTION (A+ Content / 2000 chars):

**[Brand Name] -- [Tagline or Mission Statement]**

[1-2 sentences about the brand story or philosophy]

**The Problem:**
[2-3 sentences describing the common frustration your product solves]

**The Solution:**
[2-3 sentences about how your product addresses the problem, with
specific features and benefits]

**What Makes [Product] Different:**
- [Differentiator 1]
- [Differentiator 2]
- [Differentiator 3]

**What Customers Are Saying:**
"[Short review quote]" -- [Reviewer]

**[CTA]** -- Order now and [benefit -- e.g., "start enjoying better sleep
tonight."]

---

BACKEND SEARCH TERMS (250 bytes):
[keyword1 keyword2 keyword3 synonym1 misspelling1 related-term1 ...]
```

## Template 3: Short Description (Meta Description / Snippet)

```markdown
FORMAT (150-200 chars):
[Primary benefit]. [Key feature]. [Target audience or use case].

EXAMPLES:

For a productivity app:
"Manage all your projects in one place. Kanban boards, Gantt charts, and
time tracking built in. Perfect for remote teams of 5-50."

For a skincare product:
"Visibly reduce fine lines in 4 weeks. Retinol + hyaluronic acid serum for
all skin types. Dermatologist tested. Cruelty-free."

For a kitchen appliance:
"Crispy air-fried meals in 15 minutes. 5.8 qt capacity fits a whole chicken.
One-touch presets for fries, chicken, fish, and veggies."

For a B2B tool:
"Automate invoice processing and get paid 3x faster. Integrates with
QuickBooks, Xero, and Stripe. Free 14-day trial."
```

## Template 4: Luxury / Premium Product

```markdown
# [Product Name]

*[Evocative tagline that conveys prestige or craftsmanship]*

---

[Opening: 2-3 sentences that set a scene or evoke an experience. Use
sensory language. Focus on how the product makes someone feel, not what
it does. Create desire before describing anything.]

## The [Brand] Difference

[1-2 paragraphs about what makes this exceptional. Reference
craftsmanship, materials, heritage, or design philosophy. Be specific --
"hand-stitched by artisans in Tuscany" beats "expertly crafted."]

## Crafted For

[1-2 sentences describing the discerning person this is made for.
Aspirational but not exclusionary.]

## Details

- **[Material]:** [Elegant description with provenance]
- **[Construction]:** [Process or technique that conveys quality]
- **[Finish]:** [Sensory description]
- **[Hardware/Component]:** [Premium detail]

## Dimensions & Care

| | |
|---|---|
| [Dimension 1] | [Value] |
| [Dimension 2] | [Value] |
| [Weight] | [Value] |
| [Care] | [Instructions] |

---

*[Heritage or story element -- 1-2 sentences about the brand's tradition
or the inspiration behind this piece]*

[Subtle CTA -- e.g., "Discover the collection" or "Reserve yours"]
```

## Template 5: Technical / B2B Product

```markdown
# [Product Name]

**[Clear value proposition for the business buyer -- 1 sentence]**

## Overview

[2-3 sentences: What it is, who it's for, what problem it solves. Be
direct and specific. Avoid jargon unless your audience expects it.]

## Key Capabilities

### [Capability 1 Name]
[Explanation with a specific metric or outcome. "Processes up to 10,000
transactions per second with 99.99% uptime."]

### [Capability 2 Name]
[Explanation with a specific metric or outcome.]

### [Capability 3 Name]
[Explanation with a specific metric or outcome.]

## Technical Specifications

| Specification | Value |
|---------------|-------|
| [Performance metric] | [Value] |
| [Capacity] | [Value] |
| [Compatibility] | [Value] |
| [Security] | [Value] |
| [Uptime SLA] | [Value] |

## Integrations

**Native integrations:** [List of platforms/tools]
**API:** [REST/GraphQL, rate limits, documentation link]
**Webhooks:** [Available events]

## Use Cases

- **[Use case 1]:** [Brief scenario and outcome]
- **[Use case 2]:** [Brief scenario and outcome]
- **[Use case 3]:** [Brief scenario and outcome]

## Security & Compliance

- [Certification 1 -- e.g., SOC 2 Type II]
- [Certification 2 -- e.g., GDPR compliant]
- [Certification 3 -- e.g., HIPAA eligible]

## Support

| Tier | Response Time | Channels |
|------|-------------|----------|
| Standard | 24 hours | Email, knowledge base |
| Priority | 4 hours | Email, chat, phone |
| Enterprise | 1 hour | Dedicated CSM, Slack |

---

**[CTA]** -- [e.g., "Start free trial", "Request a demo", "Talk to sales"]
```

## Template 6: Comparison / "Why Us" Description

```markdown
# Why [Product Name] vs. [Alternative]

## Side by Side

| Feature | [Your Product] | [Alternative] |
|---------|---------------|---------------|
| [Feature 1] | [Your value] | [Their value] |
| [Feature 2] | [Your value] | [Their value] |
| [Feature 3] | [Your value] | [Their value] |
| [Feature 4] | [Your value] | [Their value] |
| Pricing | [Your price] | [Their price] |

## What Customers Say

> "[Quote from a customer who switched from the alternative]"
> -- [Name, Title, Company]

## The Bottom Line

[2-3 sentences summarizing why your product is the better choice for
the target audience. Be honest -- if the alternative is better for a
different use case, say so. Credibility builds trust.]

**[CTA]** -- [e.g., "See for yourself -- start a free trial"]
```

- [Unique element 3]

- [Item 1]
- [Item 2]
