# Case Study Templates

Complete collection of case study templates for different formats and use cases.

---

## Template 1: Standard Case Study (800-1200 words)

```markdown
# How [Company] [Achieved Specific, Quantified Result]

## At a Glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Company** | [Company Name] |
| **Industry** | [Industry] |
| **Size** | [Employees or revenue range] |
| **Location** | [HQ location] |
| **Products Used** | [Your products/features] |

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| [Primary KPI] | [Impressive number] |
| [Secondary KPI] | [Supporting number] |
| [Tertiary KPI] | [Additional number] |

---

## About [Company]

[2-3 sentences about who the company is, what they do, and why they're
relatable to your target audience. Include a detail that establishes scale
or credibility.]

## The Challenge

[2-3 paragraphs describing the problems and pain points the customer faced.
Be specific about the impact -- time wasted, money lost, opportunities missed.
Show the cascading effects of the problem.]

> "[Direct quote from the customer about their challenges -- emotional,
> specific, and relatable]"
> -- [Full Name], [Title], [Company]

Key challenges included:
- [Challenge 1 -- specific and quantified if possible]
- [Challenge 2 -- specific and quantified if possible]
- [Challenge 3 -- specific and quantified if possible]

## Why [Company] Chose [Your Product]

[1-2 paragraphs about how they found your product, what alternatives they
considered, and why they chose you. Address common objections implicitly.]

## The Solution

[2-3 paragraphs describing how they implemented your product and how they
use it day-to-day. Focus on the features that drove results.]

**Key features used:**
- **[Feature 1]:** [How they use it and what it does for them]
- **[Feature 2]:** [How they use it and what it does for them]
- **[Feature 3]:** [How they use it and what it does for them]

## The Results

[1-2 paragraphs providing context for the results. When did they start
seeing improvement? What changed most dramatically?]

> "[Powerful quote about the results -- include a specific number
> if possible]"
> -- [Full Name], [Title], [Company]

### By the Numbers

- **[X]%** [improvement in primary metric]
- **[Y]x** [multiplier in secondary metric]
- **$[Z]K** [financial impact]
- **[Time]** [time saved or time to value]

### Beyond the Numbers

- [Qualitative benefit 1 -- team morale, confidence, clarity]
- [Qualitative benefit 2 -- customer satisfaction, reputation]
- [Qualitative benefit 3 -- strategic capability unlocked]

## What's Next

[1-2 paragraphs about future plans. Are they expanding usage? Adopting
new features? What's the long-term vision?]

---

**Ready to achieve similar results?** [CTA -- e.g., "Schedule a demo" or
"Start your free trial"]

[Your Company] helps [audience] [achieve outcome]. Learn more at [URL].
```

## Template 3: Metrics-Heavy Case Study

```markdown
# [Result Number] in [Timeframe]: How [Company] [Achieved Outcome]

## Dashboard

| Category | Before | After | Change |
|----------|--------|-------|--------|
| [Revenue metric] | $[X] | $[Y] | +[Z]% |
| [Efficiency metric] | [X] hrs/wk | [Y] hrs/wk | -[Z]% |
| [Quality metric] | [X]% | [Y]% | +[Z]pp |
| [Cost metric] | $[X]/mo | $[Y]/mo | -[Z]% |

**Industry:** [Industry]
**Company Size:** [Size]
**Implementation Time:** [Duration]
**Time to ROI:** [Duration]

---

## Background

[Brief company overview -- 2-3 sentences focusing on scale and operational
context that makes the metrics meaningful.]

## The Problem (Quantified)

- Lost **$[X]** annually to [specific problem]
- Spent **[X] hours** per week on [specific manual task]
- Experienced **[X]%** error/failure rate in [specific process]
- Customer satisfaction dropped to **[X]/10**

## The Approach

**Week 1-2:** [Phase 1 -- setup, configuration, data migration]
**Week 3-4:** [Phase 2 -- team onboarding, process changes]
**Week 5-8:** [Phase 3 -- optimization, scaling, refinement]

## Results Deep Dive

### [Primary Metric]: [Before] to [After]

[2-3 sentences explaining this metric, what drove the improvement, and
why it matters to the business.]

### [Secondary Metric]: [Before] to [After]

[2-3 sentences with context.]

### ROI Analysis

| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Annual investment | $[X] |
| Annual savings | $[Y] |
| Net annual benefit | $[Z] |
| Payback period | [N] months |
| 3-year ROI | [X]% |

## Customer Perspective

> "[Quote about the measurable impact and what it means for the business]"
> -- [Name], [Title]

## Key Success Factors

1. **[Factor 1]:** [Brief explanation]
2. **[Factor 2]:** [Brief explanation]
3. **[Factor 3]:** [Brief explanation]

---

[CTA]
```

## Template 4: Video Script (2-3 minutes)

```markdown
## [Company] Customer Story -- Video Script

**Total Runtime:** 2:30
**Speakers:** [Customer Name, Title]
**Location:** [Filming location]

---

### OPENING (0:00-0:15)

[VISUAL: Customer at their desk / in their workspace]
[LOWER THIRD: Name, Title, Company]

"Hi, I'm [Name], [Title] at [Company]. We [brief description of what
the company does]."

---

### THE CHALLENGE (0:15-0:45)

[VISUAL: B-roll of team working, office environment]

"Before [Your Product], we were struggling with [problem 1]. Every
[day/week/month], our team spent [X hours] just [painful task]."

[VISUAL: Relevant screen capture or illustration of the problem]

"The real cost wasn't just time -- it was [broader impact: missed
opportunities, frustrated customers, team burnout]."

"We tried [alternative 1] and [alternative 2], but nothing really
solved the core problem."

---

### THE SOLUTION (0:45-1:30)

[VISUAL: Product demo / screen recording]

"We found [Your Product] through [channel]. What stood out was
[key differentiator]."

[VISUAL: Customer using the product]

"The setup was [quick/straightforward/seamless]. Within [timeframe],
we were up and running."

"The [specific feature] has been a game-changer because [explanation
of impact]."

[VISUAL: Team collaborating with the product]

"Now instead of [old painful way], we simply [new easy way]."

---

### THE RESULTS (1:30-2:10)

[VISUAL: Data visualization / metrics on screen]

"Since implementing [Your Product], we've seen [primary result]."

[GRAPHIC: Key metric with before/after]

"Our [metric 1] went from [X] to [Y] -- that's a [Z]% improvement."

[GRAPHIC: Secondary metric]

"And [metric 2] improved by [amount], which means [business impact]."

"The biggest surprise was [unexpected benefit]."

---

### THE ENDORSEMENT (2:10-2:25)

[VISUAL: Close-up of speaker, warm tone]

"I'd recommend [Your Product] to any [role/company type] who is
[dealing with the problem]. It's [one-word descriptor] -- and the
results speak for themselves."

---

### CLOSING (2:25-2:30)

[VISUAL: Your logo + tagline + CTA]

[Your Company] -- [Tagline]
Learn more at [URL]
```
