# Ad Copy Templates

Complete collection of ad copy templates for all major digital advertising platforms.

---

## Google Search Ads

### Standard Search Ad

```
HEADLINE 1 (30 chars): [Primary keyword + benefit]
HEADLINE 2 (30 chars): [Secondary benefit or feature]
HEADLINE 3 (30 chars): [CTA or trust signal]

DESCRIPTION 1 (90 chars): [Expand main benefit. Include specific proof point. Clear CTA.]
DESCRIPTION 2 (90 chars): [Address objection or add social proof. Secondary CTA.]

DISPLAY PATH 1 (15 chars): [Keyword-rich]
DISPLAY PATH 2 (15 chars): [Category or offer]
```

**Example -- SaaS Product:**
```
H1: Cut Onboarding Time by 60%
H2: Free Trial - No Credit Card
H3: Rated #1 on G2 for 2024

D1: Automate employee onboarding in minutes, not weeks. Start your free 14-day trial today.
D2: Trusted by 5,000+ HR teams worldwide. See why companies switch to OnboardPro.

Path 1: Onboarding
Path 2: Free-Trial
```

**Example -- E-commerce:**
```
H1: Organic Dog Food - 40% Off
H2: Vet-Recommended Ingredients
H3: Free Shipping Over $50

D1: Give your dog the nutrition they deserve. All-natural, grain-free recipes. Shop now.
D2: Rated 4.9/5 by 12,000+ pet parents. Subscribe & save an extra 15%.

Path 1: Organic
Path 2: Dog-Food
```

### Responsive Search Ad (RSA)

```
HEADLINES (provide 10-15, 30 chars each):
 1. [Primary keyword + benefit]
 2. [Different benefit angle]
 3. [Question that addresses pain]
 4. [Number/stat-focused]
 5. [Social proof element]
 6. [Urgency/time element]
 7. [Price or offer]
 8. [Brand + differentiator]
 9. [How-to angle]
10. [Competitor comparison]
11. [Result customers achieve]
12. [Guarantee or risk reversal]
13. [Target audience callout]
14. [Feature highlight]
15. [Emotional appeal]

DESCRIPTIONS (provide 4, 90 chars each):
1. [Main value proposition + CTA]
2. [Feature-benefit combo + CTA]
3. [Social proof + trust signal + CTA]
4. [Urgency or scarcity + CTA]

PINNING RECOMMENDATIONS:
- Pin brand name headline to Position 1 for brand campaigns
- Pin primary keyword headline to Position 1 for non-brand
- Avoid pinning more than 2 headlines (limits Google's optimization)
```

## Google Display Ads

### Responsive Display Ad

```
SHORT HEADLINE (25 chars): [Benefit or hook]
LONG HEADLINE (90 chars): [Expanded benefit with context and proof]
DESCRIPTION (90 chars): [Value proposition + CTA]
BUSINESS NAME (25 chars): [Brand name]

IMAGE SPECS:
- Landscape: 1200x628 (1.91:1)
- Square: 1200x1200 (1:1)
- Logo landscape: 1200x300 (4:1)
- Logo square: 1200x1200 (1:1)
```

**Example:**
```
Short: Save 10 Hours/Week
Long: Marketing Teams Save 10+ Hours Per Week With AI-Powered Campaign Management
Desc: Automate your campaigns with AI. Start your free trial today.
Name: CampaignBot
```

## Facebook/Meta Ads

### Single Image Ad

```
PRIMARY TEXT (125 chars visible, up to 63K max):
[Hook sentence that stops the scroll]

[1-2 sentences expanding on the benefit or story]

[CTA line with arrow or emoji pointer]

HEADLINE (27 chars visible): [Clear benefit or offer]
DESCRIPTION (27 chars visible): [Supporting detail]
CTA BUTTON: [Learn More / Shop Now / Sign Up / Get Offer / Download]
```

**Example -- Lead Generation:**
```
Primary: 73% of marketers waste budget on the wrong channels.

Our free audit tool shows you exactly where to cut spend and where to double down. Used by 2,000+ marketing teams.

Get your free audit report -->

Headline: Free Marketing Audit
Description: Results in 60 seconds
CTA: Get Offer
```

### Long-Form Ad (Story Format)

```
[HOOK - 1 line, stop the scroll]

[PROBLEM - 2-3 lines, relate to their specific pain]

[AGITATION - 1-2 lines, make the pain feel urgent]

[SOLUTION - 2-3 lines, introduce your product naturally]

[PROOF - 1-2 lines, social proof or specific result]

[CTA - 1 line, clear next step]
```

**Example:**
```
We spent $200K on ads before we figured this out.

Every month, our team would launch campaigns, watch the budget burn, and pray for conversions. Sound familiar?

The worst part? We knew something was wrong but couldn't pinpoint what.

Then we built an attribution model that tracks every touchpoint. Now we know exactly which ads drive revenue -- and which ones just drive clicks.

Last quarter: 3.2x ROAS (up from 0.8x). Our clients are seeing similar results.

See if your ad spend is leaking --> link in comments
```

### Carousel Ad (3-5 Cards)

```
CARD 1 (Hook):
  Image: [Eye-catching visual or bold statement graphic]
  Headline (32 chars): [Problem question or bold claim]
  Description (18 chars): [Tease]

CARD 2 (Benefit 1):
  Image: [Relevant visual]
  Headline (32 chars): [First benefit]
  Description (18 chars): [Brief proof]

CARD 3 (Benefit 2):
  Image: [Relevant visual]
  Headline (32 chars): [Second benefit]
  Description (18 chars): [Brief proof]

CARD 4 (Benefit 3):
  Image: [Relevant visual]
  Headline (32 chars): [Third benefit]
  Description (18 chars): [Brief proof]

CARD 5 (CTA):
  Image: [Product shot or CTA graphic]
  Headline (32 chars): [Clear CTA]
  Description (18 chars): [Offer or urgency]
```

### Video Ad Script (15-30 seconds)

```
[0-3s] HOOK: [Bold visual + text overlay with core claim]
[3-10s] PROBLEM: [Show the pain point, relatable scenario]
[10-20s] SOLUTION: [Introduce product, show it in action]
[20-25s] PROOF: [Quick stat, testimonial flash, or result]
[25-30s] CTA: [Logo + clear CTA + offer text overlay]

CAPTIONS: Required (85% watch without sound)
ASPECT RATIO: 9:16 (Stories/Reels), 1:1 (Feed), 16:9 (In-stream)
```

## LinkedIn Ads

### Sponsored Content (Single Image)

```
INTRO TEXT (150 chars visible, 600 max):
[Professional hook addressing a B2B pain point or insight]

[Brief value statement]

[CTA]

HEADLINE (70 chars visible): [Benefit-oriented, professional tone]
DESCRIPTION (100 chars): [Supporting detail]

IMAGE: 1200x627 px
CTA BUTTON: [Learn More / Download / Register / Apply Now]
```

**Example:**
```
Intro: Sales teams using conversation intelligence close 27% more deals. New report shows how top-performing teams use AI to improve win rates.

Download the free report -->

Headline: 2025 State of AI in Sales: Benchmarks & Playbooks
Description: Based on data from 500+ B2B sales teams
CTA: Download
```

### Sponsored InMail / Message Ad

```
SUBJECT (60 chars): [Personalized + benefit-driven]

BODY (500 chars recommended, 1500 max):
Hi {{first_name}},

[Personal observation or connection -- 1 sentence]

[Value proposition -- why this matters to them -- 2 sentences]

[Social proof or credibility -- 1 sentence]

[Clear, low-friction CTA -- 1 sentence]

Best,
[Your Name]
[Title]

CTA BUTTON: [Register / Learn More / Try Free]
```

### Text Ad

```
HEADLINE (25 chars): [Benefit-focused]
DESCRIPTION (75 chars): [Value prop + CTA]
IMAGE: 100x100 px (company logo or relevant icon)
```

## Twitter/X Ads

### Promoted Tweet

```
TWEET (280 chars max, ~250 effective with link):
[Hook] + [Value statement] + [CTA] + [Link]

FORMAT VARIATIONS:

Question format:
"Still [doing painful thing]? [Product] helps you [benefit] in [timeframe]. [CTA]. [Link]"

Statement format:
"[Bold claim backed by number]. Here's how [audience] are [achieving result]. [CTA]. [Link]"

Social proof format:
"[X] companies switched to [product] this month. Result: [specific outcome]. [CTA]. [Link]"

How-to format:
"How to [achieve result] without [pain point]: [brief method]. [CTA]. [Link]"
```

### Website Card

```
TWEET TEXT (280 chars): [Compelling copy]
CARD TITLE (70 chars, ~50 visible): [Benefit headline]
IMAGE: 800x418 (1.91:1) or 800x800 (1:1)
```

## Retargeting Ad Templates

### Cart Abandonment

```
PRIMARY TEXT: Still thinking about it? Your [product] is waiting.

Complete your order today and get [offer -- e.g., free shipping / 10% off].

[CTA]

HEADLINE: Complete Your Order
DESCRIPTION: [Offer detail]
```

### Site Visitor Retargeting

```
PRIMARY TEXT: You visited [brand] recently. Here's what you might have missed:

[Key benefit or new feature they haven't seen]

[CTA]

HEADLINE: [Benefit They Missed]
DESCRIPTION: [Time-limited offer]
```

### Post-Purchase Upsell

```
PRIMARY TEXT: Thanks for choosing [brand]!

Customers who bought [product] also love [upsell product].

[Special offer for existing customers]

HEADLINE: [Upsell Offer]
DESCRIPTION: Exclusive for customers
```
