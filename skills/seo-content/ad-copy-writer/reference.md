# Ad Copy Writer -- Reference Guide

Detailed reference documentation for writing high-converting advertising copy across digital platforms.

## AIDA Framework Deep Dive

### Attention

The first 2-3 seconds determine whether a user engages or scrolls past. Attention-grabbing techniques include:

**Power Words** -- words that trigger emotional responses:
- Urgency: now, today, hurry, limited, last chance, deadline, instant, fast
- Exclusivity: secret, insider, members-only, invitation, VIP, private
- Value: free, bonus, save, discount, guaranteed, proven, certified
- Curiosity: surprising, shocking, little-known, hidden, revealed, breakthrough
- Fear: warning, mistake, avoid, never, risk, danger, don't miss

**Pattern Interrupts** -- break expectations to stop the scroll:
- Counterintuitive statements: "Stop trying to grow your email list"
- Unexpected numbers: "We lost $50K and it was the best thing that happened"
- Direct address: "You're reading this because..."
- Bold claims with proof: "3x ROI in 30 days (here's the data)"

**Question Hooks** -- engage by activating the brain's answer reflex:
- Pain-based: "Tired of wasting money on ads that don't convert?"
- Curiosity-based: "What if your CRM could close deals for you?"
- Challenge-based: "Can you afford to ignore 73% of your leads?"

### Interest

Once attention is captured, build curiosity and relevance:

- **Relevance**: connect to the reader's specific situation, role, or industry
- **Storytelling**: mini-narratives that mirror the reader's experience
- **Benefits over features**: translate what it does into what it means for them
- **Specificity**: specific numbers and details build credibility ("47% faster" beats "much faster")

### Desire

Transform interest into want through psychological triggers:

- **Social proof**: others like them are already benefiting
- **Scarcity**: limited availability creates urgency to act
- **Loss aversion**: frame what they'll miss rather than what they'll gain
- **Future pacing**: help them imagine life after using the product
- **Authority**: expert endorsements, certifications, awards

### Action

Remove friction and make the next step obvious:

- **Single CTA**: one clear action per ad
- **Action verbs**: "Get", "Start", "Claim", "Download" -- not "Click here"
- **Reduce risk**: "Free trial", "No credit card", "Cancel anytime"
- **Urgency**: time-limited offers create immediate action

## Platform Policies Reference

### Google Ads Policies

**Prohibited content:**
- Counterfeit goods
- Dangerous products or services
- Enabling dishonest behavior
- Inappropriate content

**Restricted content:**
- Alcohol (country-specific)
- Healthcare and medicines
- Financial services
- Gambling
- Political content

**Editorial standards:**
- No excessive capitalization (e.g., "FREE MONEY")
- No repeated punctuation (e.g., "Buy Now!!!")
- No gimmicky use of symbols (e.g., "F.R.E.E")
- No misleading claims
- No phone numbers in ad text (use call extensions)
- Destination must match ad content

**Trademark rules:**
- Cannot use trademarked terms in ad text without authorization
- Can bid on trademarked keywords (in most countries)
- Resellers and informational sites may qualify for exceptions

### Facebook/Meta Ads Policies

**Prohibited content:**
- Illegal products or services
- Discriminatory practices
- Tobacco products
- Unsafe supplements
- Weapons, ammunition, explosives
- Surveillance equipment
- Payday loans (with exceptions)

**Restricted content:**
- Alcohol (age-gated)
- Dating services (written approval required)
- Online gambling (written approval required)
- Cryptocurrency (written approval required)
- Social issues, elections, politics (authorization required)
- Health and wellness (no before/after images)

**Creative guidelines:**
- Text in images: no more than 20% (recommendation, not hard rule)
- No misleading or false information
- No sensational or clickbait content
- Landing page must match ad content
- No non-functional elements (e.g., fake play buttons)

### LinkedIn Ads Policies

**Prohibited content:**
- Illegal products
- Discriminatory content
- Adult content
- Weapons
- Counterfeit goods
- Multi-level marketing

**Best practices:**
- Professional tone required
- No excessive use of emojis
- No all-caps copy
- Personalization tokens allowed in sponsored InMail
- Clear advertiser identification required

### Twitter/X Ads Policies

**Prohibited content:**
- Adult content
- Illegal goods
- Hate content
- Financial scams

**Requirements:**
- Promoted tweets must comply with standard content policies
- Political advertising requires certification in some regions
- Health claims must be substantiated
- Financial products must include disclaimers

## A/B Testing Statistical Significance

### Sample Size Guidelines

To detect a meaningful difference in CTR, you need sufficient impressions per variant:

| Baseline CTR | Minimum Detectable Effect | Impressions per Variant |
|-------------|--------------------------|------------------------|
| 1% | 20% relative | ~40,000 |
| 2% | 20% relative | ~19,000 |
| 3% | 20% relative | ~12,500 |
| 5% | 20% relative | ~7,300 |

### Testing Duration

- Minimum: 7 days (to capture day-of-week variation)
- Recommended: 14-28 days
- Budget: equal spend per variant
- Significance threshold: 95% confidence (p < 0.05)

### What to Test (Priority Order)

1. **Headlines** -- highest impact on CTR
2. **CTA text** -- highest impact on conversion
3. **Primary text/description** -- moderate impact
4. **Social proof vs. no social proof** -- moderate impact
5. **Offer framing** -- moderate impact
6. **Tone/voice** -- lower impact but can be significant for brand

## Quality Score Optimization (Google Ads)

Quality Score is rated 1-10 and affects both ad position and cost per click:

| Factor | Weight | How to Improve |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| Expected CTR | ~39% | Test headlines, use strong CTAs, include keywords |
| Ad relevance | ~22% | Match ad text to keyword intent, use keyword in headlines |
| Landing page experience | ~39% | Fast load time, mobile-friendly, relevant content, clear CTA |

### Quality Score Impact on CPC

| Quality Score | Relative CPC |
|--------------|-------------|
| 10 | -50% |
| 8 | -25% |
| 7 | Baseline |
| 5 | +25% |
| 3 | +67% |
| 1 | +400% |
