# Terms of Service Writer - Reference Guide

## Enforceability Framework

### Agreement Formation Types

| Type | Mechanism | Enforceability | Court Precedent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clickwrap | User clicks "I Agree" checkbox + button | Strong | Specht v. Netscape (2002) |
| Scrollwrap | Must scroll through terms before agreeing | Moderate-Strong | Berkson v. Gogo (2015) |
| Browsewrap | Terms accessible via link, no active consent | Weak | Nguyen v. Barnes & Noble (2014) |
| Sign-in-wrap | Accepting terms as part of account creation | Moderate-Strong | Meyer v. Uber (2017) |

### Key Enforceability Factors

1. **Conspicuous notice**: Terms must be clearly visible and accessible
2. **Mutual assent**: User must take an affirmative action to agree
3. **Reasonable opportunity to review**: Terms available before agreement
4. **No unconscionable terms**: Cannot be excessively one-sided
5. **Clear language**: Ambiguous terms are construed against the drafter

## Jurisdiction-Specific Requirements

### European Union - Consumer Rights Directive

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| 14-day withdrawal right | Consumers can cancel within 14 days without reason |
| Pre-contractual information | Must provide clear info before purchase |
| Confirmation on durable medium | Email confirmation of digital content purchase |
| Unfair Terms Directive | Terms that create significant imbalance are void |
| Transparent pricing | All fees including taxes must be clear before purchase |

### California - Automatic Renewal Law

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Clear disclosure | Auto-renewal terms must be presented clearly before purchase |
| Affirmative consent | Consumer must affirmatively consent to auto-renewal |
| Acknowledgment | Provide acknowledgment including cancellation policy |
| Easy cancellation | Must provide a "simple mechanism" to cancel |
| Notice before renewal | Notify before renewal if terms have changed |

### Australia - Australian Consumer Law

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| No unfair contract terms | Standard form contract terms cannot be unfair |
| Consumer guarantees | Services must be fit for purpose, provided with care |
| Refund rights | Consumers entitled to refund for major failures |
| Cannot exclude guarantees | Consumer guarantees cannot be contracted away |

## Readability Standards

| Grade Level | Target Audience | Flesch-Kincaid Score | Characteristics |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grade 6 | General public | 80-90 | Short sentences, simple words |
| Grade 8 | Recommended for ToS | 60-70 | Clear but can use some legal terms |
| Grade 10 | Business users | 50-60 | More complex sentence structure |
| Grade 12+ | Legal professionals | Below 50 | Technical legal language |

### Readability Tips

- Average sentence length: 15-20 words
- Use active voice over passive voice
- Define legal terms in a Definitions section
- Use numbered lists instead of dense paragraphs
- Provide a plain-language summary for each section
- Break long clauses into sub-clauses with clear formatting

## Version Management

### Change Classification

| Change Type | Notice Period | Notification Method | User Action Required |
|---|---|---|---|
| Material (affects rights/obligations) | 30 days minimum | Email + in-app + banner | Affirmative acceptance recommended |
| Non-material (formatting, clarification) | 14 days | Email or in-app | Continued use as acceptance |
| Security/legal (urgent compliance) | Immediate | Email + changelog | Inform only, no action needed |
| Price increase | 30 days minimum | Direct email | Opt-in to new pricing |
