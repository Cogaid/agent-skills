## Recitals

WHEREAS, Employee is employed by Company and will have access to confidential and proprietary information in the course of their employment;

WHEREAS, Company desires to protect its Confidential Information from unauthorized use and disclosure;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of Employee's employment and access to Confidential Information, the parties agree as follows:

---

## 1. Definition of Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" includes all non-public information relating to Company's business, whether disclosed to Employee or developed by Employee during employment, including but not limited to:

a) **Trade secrets:** Proprietary processes, formulas, methods, algorithms, and techniques
b) **Technical information:** Source code, software, systems, architectures, designs, and inventions
c) **Business information:** Customer lists, supplier agreements, pricing, financial data, business plans
d) **Product information:** Roadmaps, specifications, prototypes, research data
e) **Personnel information:** Compensation data, organizational plans, employee information
f) **Marketing information:** Strategies, campaigns, market research, competitive intelligence

---

## 2. Employee Obligations

Employee agrees to:

a) Use Confidential Information solely for the benefit of Company in performing job duties;
b) Not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without prior written authorization;
c) Take reasonable precautions to prevent unauthorized disclosure, including securing physical and electronic materials;
d) Not remove Confidential Information from Company premises except as required for job duties;
e) Comply with all Company policies regarding information security and data handling.

---

## 3. Work Product Assignment

a) All inventions, discoveries, works of authorship, designs, and improvements ("Work Product") created by Employee during employment and relating to Company's business or created using Company resources are the exclusive property of the Company.
b) Employee hereby assigns to Company all rights, title, and interest in Work Product.
c) Employee will assist Company in obtaining intellectual property protection for Work Product, at Company's expense.
d) This assignment does not apply to inventions listed in **Exhibit A - Prior Inventions**.

---

## 4. Non-Solicitation

For a period of [12] months following the end of employment, Employee shall not:

a) Directly or indirectly solicit, recruit, or hire any employee or contractor of Company;
b) Solicit or attempt to solicit business from any customer or client with whom Employee had material contact during the last [12] months of employment.

---

## 5. Prior Inventions

Employee has listed all prior inventions, original works, and intellectual property in **Exhibit A**. These remain Employee's property and are excluded from the Work Product assignment.

If no prior inventions are listed, Employee represents they have no prior inventions to disclose.

---

## 6. Term and Survival

a) This Agreement is effective during Employee's employment and survives termination.
b) Confidentiality obligations continue for [2] years following the end of employment.
c) Trade secret obligations continue indefinitely, as long as the information remains a trade secret.
d) Work Product assignment is perpetual and irrevocable.

---

## 7. Exit Obligations

Upon termination of employment for any reason, Employee shall:

a) Return all Company property, documents, devices, and materials;
b) Delete all Company data from personal devices and accounts;
c) Confirm in writing that all Confidential Information has been returned or destroyed;
d) Participate in an exit interview to review ongoing obligations;
e) Sign a Separation Acknowledgment confirming understanding of post-employment obligations.

---

## 8. Remedies

Employee acknowledges that breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm to Company. Company shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief in addition to any other remedies available at law or equity.

---

## 9. General Provisions

a) **Governing Law:** This Agreement is governed by the laws of [STATE/COUNTRY].
b) **Severability:** Invalid provisions do not affect the remainder.
c) **Entire Agreement:** Supersedes all prior agreements on this subject matter.
d) **Amendments:** Must be in writing signed by both parties.
