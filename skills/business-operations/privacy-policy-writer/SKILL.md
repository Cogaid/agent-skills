# Privacy Policy Writer

Generate comprehensive, regulation-compliant privacy policies tailored to your platform, jurisdiction, and data practices.

## Purpose

This skill produces privacy policies that satisfy GDPR, CCPA, PIPEDA, and other major data protection regulations. It adapts language, disclosures, and user rights sections based on the applicable legal frameworks and the specific data practices of the organization.

## Quick Reference

### Regulation Coverage Matrix

| Regulation | Jurisdiction       | Key Requirements                              | Penalty Range            |
|------------|--------------------|-----------------------------------------------|--------------------------|
| GDPR       | EU/EEA             | Lawful basis, DPO, DPIAs, 72h breach notify   | Up to 4% global revenue  |
| CCPA/CPRA  | California, USA    | Right to know, delete, opt-out of sale         | $2,500-$7,500 per violation |
| PIPEDA     | Canada             | Consent, limited collection, accountability    | Up to $100K CAD          |
| LGPD       | Brazil             | Legal basis, DPO, international transfer rules | Up to 2% revenue         |
| POPIA      | South Africa       | Purpose limitation, info officer, conditions   | Up to 10M ZAR            |
| APP        | Australia          | 13 Privacy Principles, breach notification     | Up to $50M AUD           |

## Policy Structure Template

### Required Sections (All Regulations)

```
1. Introduction & Scope
   - Who is the data controller
   - What this policy covers
   - Effective date and last updated date

2. Information We Collect
   - Data collected directly from users
   - Data collected automatically
   - Data from third-party sources

3. How We Use Your Information
   - Primary purposes
   - Secondary purposes
   - Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

4. How We Share Your Information
   - Service providers and processors
   - Business partners
   - Legal obligations
   - Business transfers

5. Data Retention
   - Retention periods by data category
   - Criteria for determining retention
   - Deletion procedures

6. Your Rights
   - Right of access
   - Right to rectification
   - Right to erasure
   - Right to restrict processing
   - Right to data portability
   - Right to object
   - Right to withdraw consent
   - Right to lodge a complaint

7. Security Measures
   - Technical safeguards
   - Organizational measures
   - Breach notification procedures

8. International Transfers
   - Transfer mechanisms (SCCs, adequacy decisions)
   - Safeguards in place

9. Children's Privacy
   - Age requirements
   - Parental consent mechanisms

10. Cookie Policy (or link to separate)
11. Changes to This Policy
12. Contact Information
```

## Cookie Policy Template

```
COOKIE POLICY

Last updated: [DATE]

1. What Are Cookies
   Brief explanation of cookies and similar technologies.

2. Cookie Categories

   STRICTLY NECESSARY
   - Session management
   - Security tokens
   - Load balancing
   Cannot be disabled.

   PERFORMANCE / ANALYTICS
   - Google Analytics (_ga, _gid)
   - Hotjar (_hj*)
   - Internal analytics
   Require consent under GDPR.

   FUNCTIONALITY
   - Language preferences
   - Theme settings
   - Saved form data
   Require consent under GDPR.

   TARGETING / ADVERTISING
   - Facebook Pixel (_fbp)
   - Google Ads (IDE, DSID)
   - Retargeting pixels
   Always require consent.

3. Managing Your Preferences
   - Browser settings instructions
   - Consent management platform usage
   - Opt-out links for major providers

4. Updates to This Policy
```

## Compliance Checklist

### GDPR Compliance Checklist

- [ ] Identified and documented lawful basis for each processing activity
- [ ] Privacy policy is written in clear, plain language
- [ ] Cookie consent banner implemented with granular choices
- [ ] Data subject access request (DSAR) process established
- [ ] Data Processing Agreements signed with all processors
- [ ] Data Protection Impact Assessments conducted for high-risk processing
- [ ] Records of processing activities maintained (Article 30)
- [ ] Data Protection Officer appointed (if required)
- [ ] International transfer mechanisms in place (SCCs or adequacy)
- [ ] Breach notification procedure documented (72-hour window)
- [ ] Children's data handling procedures established (under 16)
- [ ] Consent mechanisms are freely given, specific, informed, unambiguous
- [ ] Privacy by design and by default implemented

### CCPA/CPRA Compliance Checklist

- [ ] "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link on homepage
- [ ] Privacy policy updated within last 12 months
- [ ] At least two methods for consumers to submit requests
- [ ] Verification process for consumer requests documented
- [ ] Financial incentive programs disclosed (if applicable)
- [ ] Service provider contracts include CCPA-required provisions
- [ ] Consumer request fulfillment within 45-day window
- [ ] Employee and B2B data handling documented
- [ ] Sensitive personal information opt-out available (CPRA)
- [ ] Annual review of data inventory completed

### PIPEDA Compliance Checklist

- [ ] Accountability principle: privacy officer designated
- [ ] Consent obtained before or at time of collection
- [ ] Collection limited to stated purposes
- [ ] Use, disclosure, and retention limited to stated purposes
- [ ] Personal information is accurate, complete, up-to-date
- [ ] Appropriate security safeguards in place
- [ ] Policies and practices are publicly available
- [ ] Individual access to their personal information enabled
- [ ] Ability to challenge compliance with privacy officer

## Plain Language Guidelines

| Instead of                                    | Write                                         |
|-----------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------|
| "We may process your personal data"           | "We use your personal information"            |
| "Pursuant to applicable legislation"          | "As required by law"                          |
| "Hereinafter referred to as"                  | "Called" or just use the name                 |
| "Notwithstanding the foregoing"               | "Even so" or "Despite this"                   |
| "Data subject"                                | "You" or "user"                               |
| "Legitimate interest"                         | "Our reasonable business need"                |
| "Sub-processor"                               | "Company that helps us provide our service"   |
| "Supervisory authority"                       | "Data protection regulator"                   |

## Workflow

1. **Intake**: Identify the platform type (website, mobile app, SaaS, IoT device)
2. **Jurisdiction scan**: Determine applicable regulations based on user locations
3. **Data audit**: Catalog all data collected, processed, and shared
4. **Third-party mapping**: List all third-party services and their data access
5. **Draft policy**: Generate sections using the structure template above
6. **Plain language pass**: Rewrite legalese into readable language
7. **Compliance cross-check**: Validate against each applicable regulation checklist
8. **Cookie audit**: Document all cookies and tracking technologies
9. **Review output**: Produce the final policy with effective date

## Best Practices

- Always include a "Last Updated" date and maintain a version history.
- Use layered notices: a short summary with links to detailed sections.
- Provide the policy in every language your product supports.
- Review and update the policy at least every 12 months or after any material change in data practices.
- Make the policy accessible (screen-reader friendly, sufficient contrast, available in multiple formats).
- Never use pre-checked consent boxes for non-essential data processing.
- Keep a changelog of policy modifications to demonstrate accountability.
- Test your DSAR process end-to-end at least quarterly.
