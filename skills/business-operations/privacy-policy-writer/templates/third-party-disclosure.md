## Data Processing Agreements

All third-party providers that process personal data on our behalf have signed Data Processing Agreements that include:

- Processing only on our documented instructions
- Confidentiality obligations for all personnel
- Appropriate security measures
- Sub-processor management with prior authorization
- Assistance with data subject rights requests
- Data deletion or return upon service termination
- Audit and inspection rights
