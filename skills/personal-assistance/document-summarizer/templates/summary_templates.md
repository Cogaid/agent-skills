# Summary Templates Collection

Select the appropriate template based on document type. Use `document_summarizer.py --template <type>` to auto-apply.

## 1. Executive Summary Template

Use for: Leadership briefings, time-pressed audiences, decision-makers.

### Format

- **Document:** [Title/Source]
- **Date:** [Date]
- **Type:** [Report/Article/Paper/etc.]

**Bottom Line:** [One sentence capturing the main point]

**Key Points:**
- [Point 1]
- [Point 2]
- [Point 3]

**Implications:** [What this means for the reader]

**Recommended Action:** [What to do with this information]

---

## 2. Article Summary Template

Use for: News articles, blog posts, opinion pieces.

- **Source:** [Publication]
- **Author:** [Name]
- **Published:** [Date]
- **Reading time saved:** [X] min to [Y] min

**Main Argument:** [1-2 sentences on the core thesis]

**Key Points:**
1. [Point 1]: [Brief explanation]
2. [Point 2]: [Brief explanation]
3. [Point 3]: [Brief explanation]

**Evidence/Data:**
- [Key statistic or fact]

**Author's Conclusion:** [What the author concludes]

---

## 3. Report Summary Template

Use for: Business reports, consultant deliverables, internal analyses.

- **Prepared by:** [Organization]
- **Date:** [Date]
- **Pages:** [X] (summarized to [Y])

**Purpose:** [Why this report was created]

**Methodology:** [How information was gathered]

**Key Findings:**
1. [Finding 1]
2. [Finding 2]
3. [Finding 3]

**Recommendations:**
1. [Recommendation 1]
2. [Recommendation 2]

**Next Steps:** [What happens after this report]

---

## 4. Research Paper Summary Template

Use for: Academic papers, scientific studies, peer-reviewed research.

- **Authors:** [Names]
- **Published:** [Journal/Conference, Date]
- **Field:** [Area of study]

**Research Question:** [What problem does this address?]

**Methodology:**
- Type: [Quantitative/Qualitative/Mixed]
- Sample: [N=X, description]

**Key Findings:**
1. [Finding with data]
2. [Finding with data]

**Significance:** [Why these findings matter]

**Limitations:** [What the study could not address]

---

## 5. Book Summary Template

Use for: Non-fiction books, textbooks, long-form published works.

- **Author:** [Name]
- **Published:** [Year]

**In One Sentence:** [The book's main message]

**Core Ideas:**
1. [Idea 1]: [2-3 sentence explanation]
2. [Idea 2]: [2-3 sentence explanation]
3. [Idea 3]: [2-3 sentence explanation]

**Actionable Takeaways:**
1. [Takeaway 1]
2. [Takeaway 2]
3. [Takeaway 3]

**Who Should Read This:** [Target audience]

---

## 6. Email Thread Summary Template

Use for: Long email chains, multi-party discussions.

- **Subject:** [Thread subject]
- **Participants:** [Names]
- **Emails:** [Number]

**Context:** [Why this thread exists]

**Decisions Made:**
- [Decision 1]

**Action Items:**

| Action | Owner | Status |
|--------|-------|--------|
| [Action] | [Name] | [Open/Done] |

**Current Status:** [Where things stand now]

---

## 7. Comparative Summary Template

Use for: Comparing multiple sources on the same topic.

- **Documents compared:** [Count]

**Comparison Matrix:**

| Dimension | Doc 1 | Doc 2 | Doc 3 |
|-----------|-------|-------|-------|
| Main argument | [X] | [Y] | [Z] |
| Key evidence | [X] | [Y] | [Z] |

**Points of Agreement:**
- [Shared conclusion]

**Points of Disagreement:**
- [Conflicting conclusion]

**Synthesis:** [Overall takeaway]
