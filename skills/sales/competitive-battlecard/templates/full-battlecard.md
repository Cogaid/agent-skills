## Trap-Setting Questions

Use during discovery to expose competitor weaknesses. Frame as genuine curiosity about the prospect's needs.

1. "How important is {{capability_they_lack}} to your workflow?"
   - **Leads to:** They cannot deliver this; we can.

2. "What is your expected timeline for implementation?"
   - **Leads to:** Their implementation takes {{longer_time}} vs. our {{shorter_time}}.

3. "Have you looked into the total cost including {{hidden_cost}}?"
   - **Leads to:** Exposes hidden fees.

4. "How does your team currently handle {{use_case}}?"
   - **Leads to:** Reveals pain point where we excel.

5. "What happens when you need to {{scale_scenario}}?"
   - **Leads to:** Exposes scalability limitations.
