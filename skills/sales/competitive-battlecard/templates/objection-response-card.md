## LAER Framework Reminder

For every objection: **Listen** (let them finish) -> **Acknowledge** (validate the concern) -> **Explore** (ask a clarifying question) -> **Respond** (counter with proof).

---

## Feature Gap Objections

### "{{competitor_name}} has {{feature_A}} and you do not."

**Explore:** "Can you help me understand how your team uses {{feature_A}} today? What specific outcome does it drive for you?"

**Respond:** "While {{competitor_name}} approaches this with {{their_approach}}, our customers have found that {{our_approach}} actually delivers better results because {{reason}}. For example, {{customer_name}} saw {{specific_result}} using our approach."

## Price Objections

### "{{competitor_name}} is cheaper."

**Acknowledge:** "Budget is always an important consideration."

**Explore:** "When you compare the pricing, are you looking at the base license, or have you factored in {{hidden_cost_1}}, {{hidden_cost_2}}, and {{hidden_cost_3}}?"

**Respond:** "When you look at total cost of ownership over 3 years, our customers typically save {{savings_amount}} because {{reason}}. {{competitor_name}}'s pricing does not include {{hidden_costs}}, which adds {{amount}} to the real cost. Would it be helpful to build a side-by-side TCO analysis for your specific situation?"

**Proof Point:** {{tco_case_study}}

### "{{competitor_name}} offered us a significant discount."

**Acknowledge:** "Aggressive discounting is common in competitive situations."

**Explore:** "Did they specify whether that discount applies to renewals as well, or is it a first-year-only offer?"

**Respond:** "We see this often. What we have found is that {{competitor_name}} typically raises prices {{amount}} at renewal, and when you factor in {{additional_cost}}, the 3-year cost is actually comparable or higher. We prefer transparent pricing that you can plan around. I am happy to share our multi-year pricing so you can compare apples to apples."

**Proof Point:** {{renewal_pricing_data}}
